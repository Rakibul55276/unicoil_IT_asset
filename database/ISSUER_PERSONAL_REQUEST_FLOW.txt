Issuer personal request rule
============================

- An active Issuer account linked to an employee can select New Equipment Request.
- The request target is SELF, so requested_for is the Issuer employee record.
- Approval follows the employee role rules already configured.
- When approved, the requesting Issuer cannot issue their own assets.
- A different active Issuer must perform issuance.
- The requesting Issuer then accepts the issued assets as receiver.
